sidebarTitle: "Channels"

---

Channels define when your Fleet agent starts running automatically. Instead of triggering your agent manually from chat, channels let it respond to real-world events — like an incoming email, a Slack message, or a scheduled time.

<Tip>
To trigger an agent on a recurring schedule rather than an event, use [schedules](https://smith.langchain.com/agents) in the Fleet settings.
</Tip>

## Add a channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agent editor">
    In [Fleet](https://smith.langchain.com/agents), open your agent and click the **Edit Agent** icon next to the agent name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the channel">
    In the **Channels** section, click **+ Add** and select the channel you want to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Follow the prompts to connect your account and authorize the channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Gmail channel

The Gmail channel activates your agent whenever a new email arrives in your primary inbox. To allow your agent to read and respond to emails, also add Gmail tools in the **Toolbox** section.

<Warning>
The Gmail channel only monitors your **primary inbox**. The following messages do not trigger it:

- Emails sent to an alias address
- Mailing list or group emails
- Messages filtered out of the inbox (spam, trash, or other folders)
</Warning>

## Slack channel

The default Slack channel activates your agent when messages are posted in a connected Slack channel. It triggers on every message in the channel and cannot receive direct messages.

<Tip>
For tag-only triggering or direct message support, connect a custom Slack bot instead. See the Fleet integrations tab for instructions.
</Tip>

## Microsoft Teams channel

The Teams channel activates your agent when messages are sent in Microsoft Teams conversations. Full setup requires an Azure Bot registration and credential configuration — see the [Fleet > Integrations tab](https://smith.langchain.com/agents/tools) for step-by-step instructions.

## Pause and resume channels

You can pause all channels on an agent without removing them:

1. Open your agent in [Fleet](https://smith.langchain.com/agents) and click **Edit Agent**.
2. In the **Channels** section, click **Pause channels**.
3. To resume, click **Resume channels**.

## Thread behavior

How conversations are marked as read depends on the trigger type:

| Trigger type | Read behavior |
|---|---|
| Chat (no channel) | Responses mark the thread as **unread** |
| Channel-based trigger | Responses keep the thread as **read** |

You can manually mark any thread as read or unread at any time.
