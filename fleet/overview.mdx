LangSmith Fleet is a no-code platform for creating and managing AI agents. You start from a template or describe your goal in plain English, connect the apps your agent needs, and let it handle routine work while you stay in control. Fleet agents can draft emails, summarize updates, monitor Slack channels, run on schedules, and more—all without touching a line of code.

<Note>
Fleet requires a LangSmith account. [Sign up here](https://smith.langchain.com/agents?skipOnboarding=true) to get started.
</Note>

## What you can build

Fleet is designed for automating everyday knowledge work:

- **Email triage**: Flag important messages, draft replies, and schedule meetings automatically.
- **Scheduled reports**: Pull data from multiple sources and deliver a daily or weekly briefing.
- **Slack and Teams bots**: Let an agent listen to a channel and respond to questions or take action on requests.
- **Cross-app workflows**: Chain tools together—read a GitHub issue, summarize it, and post an update to Slack.

## How Fleet works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for LangSmith">
    Create a [LangSmith account](https://smith.langchain.com/agents?skipOnboarding=true) to access Fleet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an agent">
    Start from a ready-to-use template, describe your goal and let AI draft the agent's configuration, or build manually from scratch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your accounts">
    Authorize the services your agent needs—Gmail, Slack, Google Calendar, GitHub, and many more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test and deploy">
    Chat with your agent to verify it behaves correctly, then activate channels or schedules to run it automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Privacy and AI usage

Fleet uses LLMs provided through the LangSmith platform. Your data is handled according to your LangSmith organization settings and [LangSmith's data retention policies](https://www.langchain.com/privacy-policy). Slack and other workspace data is not used to train LLMs.

<Warning>
Agent responses are generated by AI and may contain errors. Always verify important information before acting on it.
</Warning>
