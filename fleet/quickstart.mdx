Fleet makes it easy to create AI agents without writing code. In this quickstart, you'll use the **Email Assistant** template to build an agent that organizes your inbox — then you can test it interactively in chat.

## Before you start

You'll need:
- A LangSmith account ([sign up here](https://smith.langchain.com/agents?skipOnboarding=true))
- A Gmail account and Google Calendar
- An OpenAI or Anthropic API key

## Step 1: Get a model API key

Your agent needs an API key to connect to an AI model.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="OpenAI">
    1. Go to [platform.openai.com/api-keys](https://platform.openai.com/api-keys).
    2. Click **Create new secret key** and give it a name like "Fleet".
    3. Copy the key — it starts with `sk-`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Anthropic">
    1. Go to [console.anthropic.com/settings/keys](https://console.anthropic.com/settings/keys).
    2. Click **Create Key** and give it a name like "Fleet".
    3. Copy the key — it starts with `sk-ant-`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
Both services charge based on usage. Review pricing before proceeding.
</Warning>

## Step 2: Add your API key to LangSmith

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Go to [smith.langchain.com](https://smith.langchain.com) and click **Settings** in the bottom-left navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Secrets">
    Click the **Secrets** tab at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your key">
    Click **Add secret**, enter the key name (`OPENAI_API_KEY` or `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`), paste your key as the value, and click **Save secret**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3: Create your agent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Fleet">
    In the LangSmith UI, click **Switch to Fleet** at the top of the left-hand navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    Select **Templates** in the left navigation, then choose **Email Assistant** and click **Use this template**.

    <Tip>
    You can also start from scratch using **+ New Agent** → **Chat** (describe your agent) or **Create manually**.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Google accounts">
    Click **Connect**, sign in with Google, review the permissions, and click **Allow**. You'll be redirected back to LangSmith where your agent is created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Your agent only accesses your accounts when working on tasks you assign it. You can revoke access at any time from your Google account settings.
</Info>

## Step 4: Test your agent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the instructions">
    The template comes with a pre-filled system prompt. Read through it and make any adjustments, then click **Save changes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a test chat">
    In the right-hand panel, select the **Test Chat** tab and try a request like:

    > _Apply a "Review" label to emails that require action from me_
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve steps">
    Your agent will ask for your approval before taking actions. Click **Continue** to approve each step. Once satisfied, click **Save changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
